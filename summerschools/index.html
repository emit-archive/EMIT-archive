    EMIT Summer School

    The goal of the EMIT summer school is to provide students an overview of a growing field, opportunities to build community with other scientists, and resources for career development. The school is open to both local and visiting students, which provides an opportunity for these principles to be shared beyond our department. The school intentionally includes wellness sessions, open discussions, group projects, and breaks to model a balanced and inclusive academic atmosphere. After an overview of material and workshop sessions to learn various science tools and data analysis methods, participants spend the final week role-playing the project of attaining the first multimessenger detection of a massive black hole binary with LISA.

    Each morning begins with a session dedicated to 'Student Stories.' This is an optional window for any participant (students, speakers, organizers, supporting staff) to spend a few minutes introducing themselves in any format they prefer. A sign-up space is provided in the schedule, and students are prioritized. This gives an opportunity for a student to promote their own science, share more about their hobbies or background, or tell a personal story. We find that it helps to have organizers and local participants sign up in the first week so that they can set the tone for these informal, flexible talks.
