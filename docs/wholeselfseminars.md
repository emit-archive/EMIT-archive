# Whole Self Seminars

These seminars are intended to expand beyond the traditional science criteria and provide a more well-rounded and realistic scope of career development. Events ranged from talks from visiting professionals to group activities, discussions, and reading groups. 



#### How to conference
This workshop provided students with vital information and pointers on how to promote themselves and navigate through certain conference settings. We discussed topics and scenarios they may encounter, which prepared a lot of our students for a large upcoming event they were attending. Discussion topics included:
How attending conferences benefit you professionally and personally.
Advice for introverts or first-time conference attendees at large vs small conference settings.
Whether or not attending social outings, outside of conference agendas, are something of importance or not.
How to unwind after a day of sessions to allow oneself to detach from “work mode”, decompress and fully enjoy themselves



#### Vision Board
This session was geared towards providing an opportunity for our students to get to know each other on more of a personal level. We discussed future goals and aspirations and the attendees were provided supplies to create their own vision boards to take with them. 



#### Unmasking Oneself
This was facilitated by an esteemed Health & Wellness Practitioner/Educator and Lecturer from Cornell University, Jamilah Bey. She provided a very impactful personal/professional development  teaching our attendees ways to gain a deeper understanding of how sometimes we all wear “masks” in certain environments and provided effective ways to gain the confidence to show up in professional/social settings just by being yourself unapologetically. This workshop also allowed individuals how to have more compassion for those who have different personalities than they do.
